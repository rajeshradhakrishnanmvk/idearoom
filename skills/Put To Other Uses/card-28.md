PUT TO OTHER USES

Every idea, product, or service takes its meaning from the context in which you put it. Change the context, and you change the meaning. if you can rid yourself of your stereotypes and preconceptions and look at your subject in a fresh, innocent way, you will begin to imagine what else you could do with it.

This kind of thinking turned a fialed product into 2M's biggest consume success. the company had developed a special not-very-sticky adhesive for use on bulletin boards, a product idea that never caught on. Arthur Fry a 3M chemist, was intrigued by the glue and kept searching for other usese. One day in 1974, Fry was singing in his church choir and dropped the bookmark from his hymnal. He suddenly realized that the adhesive could be used to keep little notes and bookmarks ini place. This creative isight, that the glue's primary use should be attaching paper to paper, not to fixed surfaces, gave us the indispensable Post-It.
