Everything new is really an addition to or modification of something that already existed. To create a new idea, product, serice, process, breakthrough, or whatever else you need, just take a subject and change it into something else. The nine principal ways of changing a subject are:
Substitue something.
Combine it with something else.
Adapt something to it.
Modify or Mangnify it.
Put it to some other use.
Eliminate Something.
Reverse or Rearrange it.
