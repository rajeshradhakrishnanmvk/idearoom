Substituting one ingredient for another resulted in a favorite American dessert. The ice-cream soda was invented in the 1870s by Robert Green. In 1874, clergymen convinced the city of Philadelphia to pass a law against "sucking sodas on sunday."Green got around the ban by substituting sweet syrup for the soda, and the ice-cream sundae was born.
