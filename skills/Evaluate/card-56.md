EVALUATE


flip a coin

Use this as an intuitive method for ideas that you can evaluate with a simple yes or no.

1. Flip a coin, heads is yes, tails is no.
2. if you feel comfortable with the result, that's your decision.
3. if you feel uncomfortable with the result, make the opposite decision.

However the coin falls, your intuition makes the ultimiate decision. Charles Merrill Lynch, once said that when he makes decisions fast, he's right 60 percent of the time. IF he takes time to analyze a situation and make a more careful decision, he might be right 70 percent of the time. However, he's found that the extra 10 percent more accuracy is seldom worth the effort.
