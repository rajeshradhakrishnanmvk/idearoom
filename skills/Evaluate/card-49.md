LABEL IT
This fast-and-easy classificatios scheme is based on feasbility. 
Label each ideas as:
-Excellent(will almost certainly succeed)
-likely (needs future refinement)
-Possible chance (needs improvement)
-5/50(could go either way)
-Long shot(remote chance of success)

While you may not be interested in long-shot or 50/50 ideas, the very act of listing and classifying them expands your options. Now you have the choice of rejecting or improving them.
