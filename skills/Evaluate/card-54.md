MAP IT

1. Establish your criteria for evaluation. If you have trouble coming up witih a good list, the eight evualtion question listed on card 55 are a good starting point.
2. Foe each idea, draw a large circle on a sheet of paper.
3. Map how each ideas meets your criteria by dividing the circle into section 
4. Use difference is size, color, or design to reflect how important each aspect is to the ideas as a whole. Identify each part with a symbol or a short phrase.
5. Study the map and ask yourself: Are there any criteria I want to change? Are there some I'd like to limit or drop? Are any missing that I should include?
