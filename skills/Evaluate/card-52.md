The checkerboard

This is a technique that lets you measure each idea against a specific set of criteria.

1. Develop a list of the mos relevant criteria for screening, selecting, and supporting each idea. These might include "low cost, " "convenient,"" "nichemarket," "requires little time," and so on.
2. Write these criteria across the top of a sheett of paper
3. List all your ideas in a single column down the lef-hand side of the paper.
4. For each idea, make a check under every criterion that is to its advantage. 
5. Select the idea with the most checkmarks.

You should also try this method for rating your ideas from another perons's perspecti ve. Ask, for example, what criteria your CEO would pick, and how he or she would rate the same ideas.
