Eight Factors
As the following eight questions about each idea, awarding points according to the range indicated. On question1, for example a score of 0 means you can't communciate the idea at all, while a score of 20 means you can communicate the idea with eas. Ideas scoring 50+ points are likely to have great merit.

1. Can I communicate the idea completely and clearly? (0-20 points)
2. How much interest do I have in this idea? (0 - 20 points)
3. How good is my opportunity for implementing it? (0 - 20 points)
4. How good is the idea's timing? (0 -5 points)
5. Do I have the skills to implement this idea? (0 - 10 points)
6. Would following through on this be a good application of my personal strengths? (0 - 10 points)
7. Does this idea have good competitive advantage? (0 - 5 points)
8. How unique is this idea?(0 - 10 points)
