EVALUATE

After Walt Disney dreamed up his ideas, he would switch to the role of a realist and try to figure out how to implment them. After working that out, he would play the critic, and try to tear those ideas and solutions, apart. He spent hours looking foro weaknesses and holes. The ideas that stood up best were the ones he pursued. Playing the ciritic can help you evaluate your ideas as well.

1. For each idea, write as many criticisms as your can.
2. Next, Look at each idea and try to develop possible solutions for overcoming or repairing every weakness.
3. Select the idea that is the one with the fewest insurmoutable or irreparable weaknesses.
