1. For each idea, use a sheet of paper divided in half lengthwise. Write the idea at the toop, then label one half of the sheet "positive," the other "negative"
2. List all the positive aspects you can think of in five minutes.
3. List all the negative aspects you can think of in five minutes.
4. Assign a numerical value from 1 to 100 to every "positive" listed and from -1 to -100 for every "negative."
5. Add up the columns

Your general feelings about each idea will be clear immediatley, and the individual numbers will clarify how you feel about each item. The number of positives and negativs of each idea is less significatn than the sum of their values.
