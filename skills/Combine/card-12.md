Can you Imagine yourself merging with your subject? Einstein imagined he was a beam of light hurting through space, which led him to the theory of relativity, Imagine you are your subject, try to identify with the subject, or some part of it, to see the problem from its perspective. Ask:
Houw would I feel If I were my subject?
What would it say to me if it could talk?
What suggestions would it make? For example, what would the hands of a clock suggest for improving clocks?
